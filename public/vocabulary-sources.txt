Vocabulary sources for Chinese Revision

HSK headwords, readings, levels and item numbers follow the November 2025 HSK examination syllabus, vocabulary table, physical PDF pages 80–104, items 1–1000.
https://download.chinesetest.cn/newhsk-site/Syllabus/H_DG_1110.pdf
PDF SHA-256: d8b6646599bc9a0c9616a4cb06d4b37461f8792f70a3171016d00fcd8eda09ad

English glosses for the expanded entries are adapted from HearMandarin (https://hearmandarin.com), HSK 3.0 word list, JSON version 2026-09-09.
https://hearmandarin.com/datasets/hsk-3-0-words.json
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Glosses were selected by headword, level and reading, and edited where necessary to distinguish senses. The Chinese practice sentences were written for Chinese Revision.

Pronunciations for HSK entries 67, 678 and 181 use recordings by Chen Wang (王琛), © 2013 Wang Chen, Hugo Lopez and Nicolas Vion. Source syllables: hai2, huan2, shei2 and shui2 from Audio-cmn.
https://github.com/hugolpz/audio-cmn/tree/master/64k/syllabs
The recordings and these adaptations are licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
The clips trim room tone and apply short edge fades. The 谁 clip joins shei2 and shui2 with 400 ms of silence.

The unmodified gē and wán MP3s (HSK entries 59 and 214) are also Chen Wang recordings from Audio-cmn, under the same CC BY-SA 3.0 licence.

Source entries that link to an AISHELL-3 WAV with a time range are continuous excerpts from that recording, under the same Apache 2.0 licence. We selected word boundaries and encoded the excerpt as MP3 at the original speed. The source index identifies each speaker, original WAV, and start and end times in seconds. We checked each headword against the original transcript and the syllables against the PaddleSpeech time alignment.
